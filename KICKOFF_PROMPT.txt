GOAL: a library of paired ComfyUI workflow JSONs ("recipes") — one _low and one
_high VRAM variant per capability (text-to-video, image-to-video, text-to-audio,
TTS, image gen) — where EVERY variant, including _high, renders with peak VRAM
at or under 14.5 GB on an RTX 5080 Laptop (16 GB, Blackwell sm_120). "High" means
quality knobs up (resolution, steps, fp16 over fp8/GGUF), never over the ceiling.

RECIPES ARE FIXTURE-DRIVEN, NOT FULL PIPELINES. fixtures/ holds a frozen ledger,
scene still, character portrait, and narration wav from a real episode (read
fixtures/FIXTURES.md). Recipes contain ONLY the terminal render stage — still
and/or audio IN, video OUT. No story generation, no TTS, no still generation.
Never regenerate or replace a fixture.

TWO TEST TIERS, both driven by run_recipe.py:
- SMOKE: one single clip per recipe. Fast gate for iterating on settings.
- SUITE: a hearty multi-clip sequence run back-to-back (still sequential, one
  clip at a time): several image-to-video clips from scene_still.png, at least
  one audio-conditioned clip using narration.wav, and at least one lip-sync
  clip (portrait.png + narration.wav through the face lane). The suite records
  peak VRAM PER CLIP and flags VRAM CREEP — if peak or post-clip allocated
  VRAM climbs clip over clip, that is a FAIL even if every clip fits. Creep is
  how real multi-beat episodes die mid-render.
